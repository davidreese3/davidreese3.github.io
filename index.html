About Me

Hello! I'm David Reese, a software engineer and educator with a strong background in full-stack development, Spring Boot, and database management. I currently serve as a faculty member in the Science, Math, and Computer Science department at Marywood University, where I teach and work with students across a variety of computing topics.

I earned my Master of Science in Software Engineering from The University of Scranton in May 2025. My thesis project, RecipEase, was a recipe-sharing platform designed to help users create, explore, and share recipes easily. I also earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the University of Scranton in May 2024.

During my time at Scranton, I was heavily involved in both academics and campus life. I served as treasurer of Upsilon Pi Epsilon during my undergraduate years and later as vice president during graduate school. I also worked as a graduate teaching assistant in the Computing Sciences department, where I helped students in labs and provided academic support. Outside of the classroom, I participated in New Student Orientation, Student Government, and worked as a peer tutor.
